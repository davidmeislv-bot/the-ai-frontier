About Wire Willow

Wire Willow is a daily tech newspaper covering artificial intelligence, robotics, drones, fintech and Silicon Valley gossip.

How the daily edition works

Morning scan. Every morning, the day's tech news is swept across five desks: AI, robotics, drones, fintech and gossip.

Original drafts. Three to four stories are written up fresh — our own words, facts checked against the sources, every claim linked.

David approves. Drafts land in chat each morning. Nothing publishes without a yes — say yes, no, or send edits.

Published & archived. Approved stories go up that day and stay archived by date, so the record builds edition by edition.

Sourcing and standards

Stories are original summaries written from the linked sources. Every story links to the sources it is based on, so readers can check the originals. Where a story involves proposed spending, a private beta, or planned services, the story says so plainly rather than presenting plans as finished facts. Illustrations are original editorial artwork created for each edition.

Nothing on Wire Willow is financial advice.

Corrections

If a story needs a correction, we update the story and note what changed, with the update date shown on the page.
