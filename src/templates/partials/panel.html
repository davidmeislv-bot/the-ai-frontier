How this paper works

Scanned daily. Approved by a human.

  1. Morning scan

    Every morning, Tars sweeps the day’s tech news across five desks: AI, robotics, drones, fintech and gossip.

  2. Original drafts

    Three to four stories are written up fresh — our own words, facts checked against the sources, every claim linked.

  3. David approves

    Drafts land in chat each morning. Nothing publishes without a yes — say yes, no, or send edits.

  4. Published & archived

    Approved stories go up that day and stay archived by date, so the record builds edition by edition.
