Walmart is about to let an AI assistant do the shopping. The retailer is rolling out a live version of its Agent Gateway — announced on October 21 — on Tuesday, November 24, with Meta’s Muse as the first agent. Black Friday sales run through November 29.

Muse can find products, build a basket, and complete purchases on Walmart.com and Sam’s Club through a direct connection using the Model Context Protocol, rather than by driving a browser the way earlier shopping agents did. Purchases run through dedicated “Agent Profiles” inside the customer’s Walmart account, where shoppers set spending limits, approve purchases, and review the agent’s buying history. Checkout uses a card saved in the Walmart account, at the same prices as Walmart.com, under Walmart’s normal return policy. The Gateway is designed to let agents find relevant products without sharing the shopper’s purchase history.

The setup has three steps: tell Muse what you need, such as a television deal or Black Friday deals available for delivery; set shopping rules — Muse takes you to Walmart to sign in, and the first item added to a cart triggers the Agent Profile setup with a spending limit; then check out inside the conversation.

Walmart says Muse support is “just the beginning,” with more agents and apps to come. The open door stands out against the rest of the industry: Amazon and eBay block AI agents from buying on their platforms, and Amazon has blocked Meta’s Muse from purchasing on its marketplace.
