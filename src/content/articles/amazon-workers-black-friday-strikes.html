Amazon workers and their supporters plan strikes and protests in more than 20 countries over the Black Friday and Cyber Monday weekend, in the seventh year of the Make Amazon Pay campaign.

The actions run from Friday, November 27, to Monday, November 30, and target Amazon during its busiest sales weekend of the year. The campaign, convened by the trade union federation UNI Global Union and Progressive International, is demanding fair wages, safer working conditions, union recognition and respect at work, and is pressing Amazon to negotiate with unions.

In Germany, the ver.di union called strikes at 11 Amazon sites — the most it has ever called at once — beginning early Friday morning, including Leipzig, Bad Hersfeld, Dortmund, Werne and Rheinberg. In the United States, the Teamsters will hold rallies and press conferences on Cyber Monday in Los Angeles and Chicago. In India, the Amazon India Workers Union and allied unions plan rallies in at least 23 locations, including Delhi and Mumbai. Further actions are planned in Canada, Australia, Japan, Brazil, South Africa and elsewhere.

The campaign points to a recent win: on November 13, an independent arbitrator set the terms of a three-year collective agreement for about 800 workers at Amazon's YVR2 fulfillment center in Delta, British Columbia, represented by Unifor — the first binding union contract for Amazon workers anywhere in North America.
