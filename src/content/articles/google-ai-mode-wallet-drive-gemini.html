Google is letting its Search AI Mode draw on more of your personal data — starting with your wallet. From today, users can opt in to connect Google Wallet to Personal Intelligence in AI Mode, with support for Drive and the Gemini app to follow in the coming weeks. The three join Gmail, Calendar, and Photos, which could already be connected.

With Wallet connected, AI Mode can map out financial milestones, suggest ways to trim everyday spending, and help maximize credit-card benefits such as cash-back categories, travel perks, and reward points, based on the cards linked through Wallet. Google says the access is read-only: “If you choose to connect your Wallet, AI Mode can only read information from your accounts; it can’t move, manage, or invest your money.”

Drive support will let AI Mode take documents, spreadsheets, and PDFs into account — Google’s example is a personalized preparation plan ahead of tax season. The Gemini app connection will share context in both directions, so an idea explored in Gemini can become the starting point for an AI Mode response, and vice versa. That connection will give AI Mode access to Gemini chats, shared photos, files, audio and video attachments, and associated insights and locations.

Google will soon show a Personalization icon below tailored responses that lets users see the context AI Mode used, and users can adjust or turn off personalization at any time in Search Settings. The announcement says nothing about advertising, and Google has not said whether the newly connected data will also shape the ads shown beside AI Mode answers.
