Google is rolling out new Gemini settings that separate chat history from AI model training, so users can keep their conversation history while opting out of having those chats used to train Google’s models.

Until now, one setting — Keep Activity — controlled both. Google is replacing it with two: a History setting that controls how long chats are saved, with an adjustable retention period, and a separate AI model training setting that controls whether chat content, including text and attachments, is used to train generative AI models. Training is opt-out: users need to find the toggle under Activity in Gemini settings, or in their Google account under Data and Privacy, then My Activity, then Other activity, then Gemini Apps Activity. The new settings inherit the current Keep Activity choice when the change reaches an account.

There is one qualification. Turning the new History setting off does not mean chats disappear immediately: Google says future chats will still be saved for around two months for service, feedback, and safety purposes, and human reviewers may help with those purposes.
