OpenAI is folding ChatGPT's chat and work tools into a single product — and a single usage limit.

The company has started rolling out a unified Chat and Work experience in ChatGPT to a small group of Pro subscribers, product lead Thibault "Tibo" Sottiaux said in a post on X on Tuesday. Under the new system, Chat and Work will share one usage meter across ChatGPT. OpenAI plans to bring the shared limits more broadly in December, according to its support page.

An OpenAI spokesperson told TechCrunch that most users are not expected to face more frequent usage resets, but a small fraction of the heaviest users may hit their limits sooner. Conversations using the Instant model will remain unlimited and will not count toward the shared allowance, and users can keep using Instant after exhausting their limits.

Some subscribers have complained on social media and OpenAI's community forum that merging the limits could leave them with less access to advanced reasoning models while they keep paying the same subscription fees.

The change lands as OpenAI also corrects a billing error from the GPT-6.2 launch that had inflated users' effective limits. Sottiaux said on X that with the fix, users will reach their caps 40 percent faster on the Astra model and 25 percent faster on Sol.

Rival Anthropic made a similar move in September, bringing its conversational service Claude and its work-focused Cowork tool under one interface. The companies face the same pressure: demand for more capable models keeps rising while the computing power behind them gets more expensive.
