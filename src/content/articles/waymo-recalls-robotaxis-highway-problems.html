Waymo is rolling out two software recalls across its fleet of 3,871 robotaxis, after a run of highway incidents that led California to ban the company's cars from freeways.

The updates will stop the vehicles from entering closed highway construction zones, and improve how they respond to police and emergency crews directing traffic. The move follows several incidents involving construction breaches and ignored officer signals.

California's freeway ban forced Waymo to pause highway operations nationwide while it fixes the problems. The recalls show the flip side of a software-defined fleet: a problem found on the highway can be patched everywhere at once — but a ban can ground it everywhere too.
