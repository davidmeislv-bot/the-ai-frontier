This is a press-release story. Doodle Labs and XTEND announced on Nov. 13 that they are expanding their partnership, integrating Doodle Labs Mesh Rider radios with XTEND’s XOS software. According to the release, that integration is complete on the STRIKER drone.

The release’s central promise is about staying connected in hard conditions. The companies claim the setup keeps links connected through interference and electronic warfare, and supports operating in GNSS-denied environments. Those are the companies’ claims in their press release, presented here as claims — not as independently tested findings.

The release also gives the partnership a concrete backdrop: XTEND was awarded a $40.5 million order for 6,000 STRIKER systems, announced Oct. 13. That order is the scale against which this connectivity work will roll out.

What to watch is the STRIKER rollout itself — how widely the Mesh Rider and XOS integration ships across those systems, and what operators report once it is in use.
