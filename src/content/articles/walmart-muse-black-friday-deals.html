Walmart's Black Friday deals can now be shopped by an AI agent.

The retailer announced on Tuesday that customers can shop its Black Friday deals through Muse, Meta's personal AI assistant. Shoppers tell Muse what they need — say, a television for under $500 or the season's most-wanted toys — and the assistant finds matching products, builds a basket and completes the purchase inside the conversation.

The connection runs through the Walmart Agent Gateway, a system the retailer launched in October that lets outside AI assistants connect directly to its online store. Muse is the first agent to use it. The setup works differently from ordinary web shopping: Muse passes the shopping list to Walmart, and Walmart picks the products that come back.

Shoppers set the rules through Agent Profiles in their Walmart account, including a spending limit, which orders need approval and a log of everything the agent bought. The profile can be deleted at any time, and Walmart says Muse does not see a customer's broader purchase history. Checkout uses a card already saved in the Walmart account, at the same prices as Walmart.com. Signing in happens on Walmart, not inside Meta's app.

Walmart also upgraded its own assistant, Sparky, which can now turn a handwritten shopping list into a cart and use images and voice to find products. Black Friday falls on November 27.
