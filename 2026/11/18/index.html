In Brief

Three stories in brief from Wednesday, November 18, 2026 — a $700 million raise for Ukraine-tested drones and robots, Microsoft's specialist AI models, and a bipartisan bill for a cyber and AI investigations board.

Drones · Funding

UFORCE raises $700M to build Ukraine's drone arsenal at scale

UFORCE, the London-headquartered, Ukrainian-founded maker of uncrewed sea, air, and land systems, raised a $700 million Series A at a $6 billion valuation on November 12, co-led by Valor Equity Partners and the UK National Wealth Fund, which put in £113 million (about $150 million) — the fund's largest defense investment to date — with the U.S.–Ukraine Reconstruction Investment Fund also taking part. The company says its MAGURA sea drones contributed to the destruction of more than a dozen Russian warships, and its NEMESIS strike drones have flown more than 300,000 combat flights; those figures are the company's own and have not been independently verified. No revenue was disclosed.

Sources: Modern Robotics, Nov 13 · TechTimes, Nov 14

AI

Microsoft's Ignite brings specialist AI models for coding, security, and small decisions

At Ignite, Microsoft announced MAI Code 2 for agentic coding, MAI Cyber 2 Flash with 12 billion active parameters for cybersecurity, and Microsoft-Decision-1.1 for routing, classification, and verification tasks too small for a large general model. It also introduced Agent 365 Business Analytics to tie AI spending to business outcomes. Separately, Microsoft said on November 13 it will switch the small language model built into Windows from Phi Silica to the 2.3-billion-parameter Aion Instruct.

Sources: EPC Group, Nov 18 · Microsoft Dev Blogs

Policy

After a week of outages, a bipartisan bill would create a board to investigate AI and cyber failures

Reps. Morgan Luttrell (R-Texas) and Ritchie Torres (D-N.Y.) introduced the ROOT CAUSE Act on Wednesday to establish a National Cyber and AI Investigations Board inside CISA, to determine why significant incidents happened and recommend ways to prevent them from recurring. The bill follows a nationwide FAA ground stop that delayed more than 10,000 flights, a Microsoft outage that disrupted workplace tools, and an outage on the NSA's classified NSANet network. A companion proposal, the AI INSIGHT Act from Rep. Matt Van Epps, would direct CISA to help agencies identify AI agents on federal networks and limit their access.

Source: Nextgov/FCW, Nov 18
