Google pitches Gemini 4 Hydrogen for coding agents

Announced Nov. 12, Gemini 4 Hydrogen is Google’s faster, lower-cost pitch for everyday coding and agentic work — $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens across a 1 million-token window. The performance claims are Google’s own, not independently verified.

By Wire Willow Staff · Nov 13, 2026 · 3 min read

Google used Nov. 12 to introduce Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a model it is positioning for the daily work of software teams: everyday coding tasks and agentic workflows, where a model does not just answer once but works through a series of steps.

The pitch leans on speed and price. Google lists Gemini 4 Hydrogen at $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens, across a 1 million-token window — a window large enough to hold substantial code and context in a single run.

One caution belongs right next to the headline numbers. The performance and speed claims in the announcement — including Google’s claim that Hydrogen beats Argon on an intelligence index — are Google’s own claims in its announcement. They have not been independently verified, and this story reports them as the company’s claims, not as settled results.

What to watch is adoption: whether developers actually route everyday coding and agent work to Hydrogen at those listed prices, and whether outside testing backs up the company’s account of its speed and capability.
