SpaceX proposes more than $74 billion for two AI supercomputing campuses

SpaceX says it plans to put more than $24 billion into Starbase, Texas and roughly $50 billion into Cochise County, Arizona. This is proposed spending from a company statement — not money spent or built yet — and both sites still need local decisions on Dec. 9.

By Wire Willow Staff · Nov 13, 2026 · 4 min read

The story in 30 seconds

  • Proposed total: More than $74 billion across two AI supercomputing campuses, according to a SpaceX statement reported by Reuters.
  • Split: More than $24 billion at Starbase, Texas and roughly $50 billion in Cochise County, Arizona.
  • Not yet built: This is proposed spending, not money spent or construction completed, and both sites still need local decisions on Dec. 9.
  • Dec. 9 decisions: A Cochise County zoning hearing and a Starbase tax-abatement vote.

SpaceX laid out a very large building plan on Thursday: more than $74 billion in total for two AI supercomputing campuses. In the company’s statement, more than $24 billion of that total is tied to Starbase, Texas, and roughly $50 billion is tied to Cochise County, Arizona.

The wording matters here. This is proposed spending from a company statement reported by Reuters — not money that has been spent, and not campuses that have been built. Nothing in the announcement turns the proposal into a finished project on its own.

Both sites still have a local gate to clear, and both decisions land on the same day. On Dec. 9, Cochise County is set to hold a zoning hearing on the Arizona plan, and Starbase faces a tax-abatement vote. What happens in those two rooms will shape whether, and how, the proposal moves forward.

The two sites are described differently. The Arizona plan is described as reaching up to 6 GW at full build-out, using dry cooling and with no planned industrial groundwater use. The Starbase site, by contrast, would generate its own electricity.

For now, the story is the scale of the proposal and the Dec. 9 decisions in front of it. Wire Willow will be watching both local outcomes before treating any part of this $74 billion plan as underway.
