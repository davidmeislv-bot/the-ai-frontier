SpaceX proposes more than $74 billion for two AI supercomputing campuses

SpaceX says it plans to put more than $24 billion into Starbase, Texas and roughly $50 billion into Cochise County, Arizona. This is proposed spending from a company statement — not money spent or built yet — and both sites still need local decisions on Dec. 9.

By Wire Willow Staff · Nov 13, 2026 · 4 min read

Read the full story →

Illustration: Wire Willow. Two desert campuses, one very large proposal — and two local votes still to come on Dec. 9.

The story in 30 seconds

  • Proposed total: More than $74 billion across two AI supercomputing campuses, according to a SpaceX statement reported by Reuters.
  • Split: More than $24 billion at Starbase, Texas and roughly $50 billion in Cochise County, Arizona.
  • Not yet built: This is proposed spending, not money spent or construction completed, and both sites still need local decisions on Dec. 9.
  • Dec. 9 decisions: A Cochise County zoning hearing and a Starbase tax-abatement vote.

Three more things

Agile Robots closes a $500M Series F

Agile Robots closed a $500 million Series F led by SoftBank at an $8.5 billion valuation. Nvidia, Hitachi and T.Capital also took part, bringing the company’s total funding to about $2 billion.

Sources: Agile Robots · SiliconANGLE, Nov 12

Griffin raises $20M to add cards and stablecoin services

Griffin, a UK licensed bank for fintechs, raised $20 million in a Series B announced Nov. 12, led by Octagon Partners with Notion Capital, A+ Ventures, Nordic Ninja and Breega taking part, at a valuation above $100 million. The plan is to expand into card issuing — branded retail and corporate debit cards, with credit to follow — and stablecoin services, including reserve services for stablecoin businesses and on-chain cross-border payments. Those are planned services, not launched, and no rollout schedule was given.

Sources: CoinDesk, Nov 12 · PYMNTS

Anthropic opens a private beta for self-hosted Claude Managed Agents

Anthropic opened a private beta on Nov. 12 that lets companies run the Claude Managed Agents service — the harness, sandboxes, credentials and session history — in their own cloud or on-premises. The model itself does not move: model requests still go to Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, Google’s Agent Platform, Microsoft Foundry, or the customer’s own gateway. Access is by request through an Anthropic account team, with no pricing or general-availability date given. This is a private beta only — not a general release and not a fully offline Claude. The same day, Anthropic launched Claude for Manufacturing for Team and Enterprise plans, with 17 skills and connectors for Autodesk Fusion, Infor, Microsoft 365 and Oracle NetSuite.

Sources: AI Insiders · GE Roundtable
