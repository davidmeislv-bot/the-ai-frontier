Anthropic’s IPO is now $93.5 billion — the greenshoe took it past the record

Underwriters bought every extra share on offer, and a $26 billion private placement took the total raised to $119.5 billion.

By Wire Willow Staff · Nov 23, 2026 · 1 min read

The story in 30 seconds

  • The close: Anthropic closed its IPO on Friday after underwriters fully exercised their option for extra shares, lifting gross proceeds from $81.3 billion to $93.5 billion.
  • The shares: The offering sold 1.127 billion Class A shares at $83 apiece — 980 million in the base offering plus 147 million through the overallotment. The extra shares added about $12.2 billion.

Anthropic closed its initial public offering on Friday after underwriters fully exercised their option to buy extra shares, lifting the gross proceeds from $81.3 billion to $93.5 billion. The offering sold 1.127 billion Class A shares at $83 apiece — 980 million in the base offering plus 147 million through the overallotment, commonly called the greenshoe. The extra shares added about $12.2 billion, with no added commission. Of the $93.5 billion, about $91.6 billion went to Anthropic; the rest went to existing shareholders who sold stock.

Alongside the public offering, Anthropic closed a concurrent private placement of Class N shares at the same $83 price, worth about $26 billion, bought by seven technology companies: AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia, and TSMC. Together, the public offering and the private placement brought in $119.5 billion before fees — the largest IPO on record by cash raised, passing SpaceX’s June listing, the previous record holder.

The stock itself has cooled since the debut. Shares opened at $111.50 on Thursday and closed the first session at $97.17, up 17% from the offer price, then fell 8.5% on Friday to $88.91 amid a broader technology sell-off.
