Nothing’s first AI gadget is a pair of earbuds that records your meetings and your calls

The Ear (note) hides microphones in its charging case as well as the buds, with AI transcription and summaries in a companion app. It launches December 8; the price is still unannounced.

By Wire Willow Staff · Nov 23, 2026 · 1 min read

The story in 30 seconds

  • The product: The Ear (note) is Nothing’s first AI gadget: wireless earbuds that record and transcribe conversations, fully unveiled on December 8.
  • The hardware: The charging case has built-in microphones and a “Talk” button for in-person meetings; the earbuds record phone calls. A companion app handles transcriptions and AI summaries.
  • The caution: Co-founder Carl Pei told Bloomberg he does not expect AI gadgets like this to succeed in the mass market anytime soon.
  • The price: Pricing, specifications, and availability are expected at the December 8 event; none has been announced.

Nothing’s first device for the AI era is a pair of wireless earbuds that record and transcribe conversations. The London phone maker confirmed to Bloomberg that the product, called the Ear (note), will be fully unveiled on December 8.

The recording hardware is split in two. The charging case has built-in microphones and a “Talk” button, letting it record in-person meetings; the earbuds themselves can record phone calls. A companion app manages the recordings, transcriptions, and AI-generated summaries. The buttons and microphones are programmable, so they could also be used to control third-party software rather than only Nothing’s own app.

The earbuds will also support interactions with various AI agents, including a basic voice-control system developed by Nothing that supports back-and-forth conversation. What those agent interactions will actually do has not been detailed.

Co-founder Carl Pei told Bloomberg he does not expect AI gadgets like this to succeed in the mass market anytime soon. In the medium term, Nothing aims to become a major provider of hardware that supports a wide variety of AI agents rather than being tied to specific models. Pricing, specifications, and availability are expected at the December 8 event.
